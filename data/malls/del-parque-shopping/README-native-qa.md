# Del Parque Shopping — raster-source traced geometry (provisional)

The source images have 2048 × 1529 pixels and are archived in original-byte base64 chunks; registered display WebP files preserve this native resolution. The SIFT registration to the previous common reference frame has an inlier reprojection residual <1.6 old pixels at P95. This proves image alignment to prior reference pictures, NOT physical floor alignment.

Only 33 individually enclosed green-outline spaces are promoted to the 3D visual *candidate* layer. Eight additional printed LOCAL labels are intentionally left without 3D shapes rather than closing unobserved walls or substituting an old approximate rectangle. Their stable IDs survive in PB/P1/P2 closed-face-candidates.json files.

The original dark green strokes are archived as SVG traced directly from the pixel segmentation. The registered full-resolution image is the end-user comparison source. Yellow/orange/cyan/purple source facilities are independently tagged as colored draft regions when large/closed enough.

Not a verified directory, cadastral survey, GPS indoor map or routing graph. No official store names, codes, entrances, walls, altitudes, floor heights or connections should be inferred. QA remains pending and the mall is non-navigable.
